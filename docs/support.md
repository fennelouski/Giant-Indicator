# Giant Indicator support

Giant Indicator makes battery, power, volume, connectivity, time, date and optional weather readable at a glance.

- Open Settings by double-clicking or double-tapping the dashboard. On Mac, use the Settings toolbar button or press Command-comma. On Apple Vision Pro, use the Settings button.
- Select a settings section, then choose your indicators or display options. Your choices remain saved when you close the window or relaunch the app.
- Tap or click the battery percentage to cycle its display styles.
- Weather requires optional location access and an internet connection. Continue through the in-app permission explanation to request access. A denied permission never prevents use of the other indicators.
- Wi-Fi network names depend on device support and location permission. Mac Wi-Fi signal strength requires an active Wi-Fi interface. Some audio devices have a fixed volume; the volume indicator reports an unavailable state when the device cannot expose a level.
- A desktop Mac without a battery reports battery information as unavailable. Select Time, Date, Wi-Fi or Volume to make a useful dashboard for that Mac.
- macOS and visionOS manage display brightness and system status presentation. Giant Indicator offers these controls only on supported iPhone and iPad displays.

For help, bug reports or privacy questions, [contact Nathan Fennel](https://nathanfennel.com/contact). Include your device, operating system version and a description of what happened. Do not include sensitive information in screenshots or reports.
