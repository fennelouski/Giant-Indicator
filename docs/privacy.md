# Giant Indicator privacy policy

Giant Indicator displays your device status, time, date and optional local weather. It does not require an account and includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

## On your device

Your dashboard choices, display preferences and permission education choices are saved locally until you change them. A local cache stores weather conditions and Apple Weather attribution so a recent forecast can remain useful when a connection fails. Giant Indicator does not upload these preferences or device status to a developer server, and it does not synchronize them through iCloud.

## Optional local weather and Wi-Fi network name

The app asks for location access only after you choose a feature that needs it and continue through the permission explanation. Local weather uses Apple's WeatherKit service. Your device’s location is sent to Apple to request conditions and forecasts. Giant Indicator requests kilometer-level accuracy from the operating system; the location returned by the device may be more accurate. Apple processes WeatherKit requests under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer does not receive your location or operate a weather server.

If you enable the Wi-Fi network name on a supported device, the app uses the location permission required by the operating system to read that name. The network name is displayed on your device and is not sent to the developer or included in the weather request.

You can disable weather or network-name display in Giant Indicator's Settings. You can revoke location access in your device's privacy settings. The app remains usable without location access.
